My journey in computing began in 2017, and since then I have pursued increasingly challenging problems at the intersection of theory and application. As a Computer Engineering student at HBKU with a 3.97 GPA, I have had the privilege of engaging with Qatar's research ecosystem through internships at QCRI and QEERI, contributing to projects that bridge academic inquiry with real-world impact. These experiences, combined with my coursework and independent research, have solidified my interest in pursuing graduate study in Data Science and Engineering at HBKU.
My research experience spans both theoretical and applied domains. My publication in SIAM Undergraduate Research Online, "Evaluating Combinatorial Approaches to Nested Loop Counting and Their Generalizations," reflects my interest in rigorous analytical problem-solving. On the applied side, I contributed to FADA (Fetal Anomaly Detection Algorithm) as part of the AI4GH Innovation Hub, a collaborative effort focused on using artificial intelligence to improve maternal health outcomes in the MENA region. Working on machine learning models for fetal ultrasound analysis taught me how computational methods can directly serve healthcare, which is a lesson that continues to shape my research interests.
Beyond these projects, I have developed strong interests in cybersecurity, hands-on electronics, and agentic AI systems. I am particularly drawn to how autonomous AI agents can augment human decision-making in complex environments. This intersection of machine learning, systems thinking, and security aligns naturally with the Data Science and Engineering program's emphasis on both foundational methods and practical applications.
HBKU has been central to my academic development. Having taken courses with faculty in this program, I have experienced firsthand the quality of mentorship available here. The university's position within Education City provides unmatched access to collaborative research opportunities, something I have already benefited from and hope to continue leveraging as a graduate student. My recognition as a QRDI Rising Innovator further motivates me to contribute to Qatar's growing research landscape.
I am eager to deepen my expertise in data science while exploring its applications in areas such as AI safety, autonomous systems, and healthcare. I look forward to contributing to HBKU's research community and continuing the work I have started here.
